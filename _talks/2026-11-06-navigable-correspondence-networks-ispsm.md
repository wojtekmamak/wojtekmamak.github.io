A version of the talk first given at [The Architecture of Propositional Thought](/talks/navigable-correspondence-networks) (University of Missouri, October 2026), presented in the symposium *The Navigational Mind*.

I argue that Representational Similarity Analysis, interpreted within a Correspondence Network Framework, bridges the gap between map-like (grid and distance) codes in hippocampal–entorhinal and frontal cortices and propositional content: these codes are best understood as navigable correspondence networks, over which propositional operations can be modelled as constrained trajectories and evaluations.
